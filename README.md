# Coincidence

If you make up a sequence of a hundred Heads or Tails choices, can a detector
tell you apart from a fair coin?

[Play Coincidence](https://jldc.github.io/coincidence/)

Choose Heads or Tails 100 times. Coincidence compares your sequence with
human-generated choices and independent fair-coin flips, then shows how
human-like it looks alongside a fresh random sequence.

## Privacy

The detector runs in your browser. Coincidence does not send or keep your
choices, score, result, or reaction time. Read the [privacy
notice](https://jldc.github.io/coincidence/#privacy) for the hosted site's
anonymous page and performance measurement.
